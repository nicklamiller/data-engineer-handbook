**Product:** Spotify

**User journey:**



* **Impressions**
    * While there are alternatives, Spotify has the majority of the market and it shows in word of mouth impressions. The vast majority of my friends and people I follow online almost always share their music using Spotify links. This social proof made me confident that it was a solid, well-regarded music streaming service.
* **Sign ups/Emails Acquisitions**
    * Signing up was effortless. The process was quick and there weren’t spammy emails prompting me to complete the registration. Spotify seems to know they don’t need to be overly persistent with new users since their brand is so pervasive and has a very good reputation.
* **Purchase**
    * Upgrading to Premium was extremely easy and I had no complications. The value proposition was clear, and the checkout process was seamless.
* **Engaged/Retained**
    * Initially, the core service was enough to keep me retained. The improved UI, vast music selection, access to user-created playlists, and pre-loading feature for continuous playback were significant upgrades from my previous free music app. However, my long-term engagement has been solidified by Spotify's personalization features. **Discover Weekly** quickly became an essential tool for finding new artists I genuinely love, making the platform feel like it truly understands my taste. Furthermore, the annual **Spotify Wrapped** feature has become a fun, nostalgic, and highly shareable event that deepens my personal connection to the service and my listening habits throughout the year.
* **Referral/Testimony**
    * I’ve enjoyed using the app so much that I have gifted Spotify Premium memberships to family members who enjoy music, acting as a direct advocate for the service.

**3 experiments and hypotheses:**

**1. Improving Playlist Shuffle Behavior After Filtering**

Making the default behavior of shuffling through a playlist such that initially filtering songs does not limit the set of songs that can be shuffled through. For example, searching a playlist for songs that start with an “A” and selecting a song should still result in the default shuffle behavior going through all songs in the full playlist, not just the songs returned by the search.



* **Test Allocation & Groups**
    * **Group 1 (Control - 50% of users):** Default behavior where searching/filtering a playlist relegates the set of songs played via shuffle to only the filtered results.
    * **Group 2 (Test - 50% of users):** Searching/filtering a playlist does not change the underlying set of songs that can be played when shuffle is activated; shuffle will always play from the entire playlist.
* **Hypothesis**
    * **Leading metrics:** We hypothesize that for Group 2, users will have a less frustrating experience. This will manifest as an increase in *average session duration* and a decrease in the *frequency of playback stops/skips* within the first 30 seconds of starting a shuffle play from a filtered list. We will also monitor the *number of times a user has to exit a filtered view to restart shuffle*, expecting it to decrease.
    * **Lagging metrics:** We hypothesize that a more intuitive shuffle feature will increase long-term user satisfaction. This will lead to an increase in *successful referrals* to users on other services (like Apple Music, where this is the default behavior), as this small point of friction will have been removed.

**2. Allowing Gift Cards to Apply Directly to Existing Premium Accounts**



* **Test Allocation & Groups**
    * **Group 1 (Control - 50% of users):** Default behavior where gift cards cannot be easily applied to existing, active Premium accounts, often requiring users to cancel their subscription or contact support.
    * **Group 2 (Test - 50% of users):** Gift cards can be directly applied to existing Premium accounts as a credit towards future billing cycles through a simple redemption flow.
* **Hypothesis**
    * **Leading metrics:** We hypothesize this change will streamline the user experience. For Group 2, we expect a significant decrease in the *volume of customer support tickets* related to gift card redemption.
    * **Lagging metrics:** We hypothesize that a frictionless gifting process will build goodwill. This will lead to an increase in *user referrals for gift cards* and an improved *premium subscription renewal rate* for users in Group 2 who redeem a gift card.

**3. Creating an easier gateway for artists who primarily publish on SoundCloud/YouTube**

This experiment proposes integrating content from platforms like SoundCloud and YouTube, which host a vast amount of music from emerging artists not yet on Spotify. Recognizing the significant legal and licensing constraints, an initial test could be performed with a curated set of independent artists who have given explicit permission.



* **Test Allocation & Groups**
    * **Group 1 (Control - 50% of users):** Normal Spotify selection.
    * **Group 2 (Test - 50% of users):** Users have access to all standard Spotify content, plus an integrated catalog of music from a select group of partner artists from SoundCloud/YouTube.
* **Hypothesis**
    * **Leading metrics:** We hypothesize that access to a wider and more diverse content library will increase engagement. For Group 2, we expect to see an increase in *time spent Browse and searching* on the app and a higher *number of unique artists streamed per user*.
    * **Lagging metrics:** Over the long term, we predict this will increase the platform's value proposition. We expect an increase in *overall user satisfaction scores (NPS)*. Crucially, we would also monitor the *number of new, independent artists who apply to be part of this expanded catalog program*, indicating its value to the creator ecosystem and strengthening Spotify's position as the go-to platform for all audio.
